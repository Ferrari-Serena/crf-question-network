2. A line between two points = "these two questions share the same root"

A line is not "this question causes that one" (not causal), but "these two are two sides of the same thing". Composed of two signals:

  • Shared tags (60%): same Nobel theory / same citation / same seed question / same governance or constraint dimension
  • Shared wording (40%): semantic similarity of English wording (TF-IDF)

Hover or click any node — the right panel lists "why linked (shared tags / citations)", which is the evidence for that line.

3. Why some clusters mix together

Clusters sit close together when they describe the same mechanism from different stakeholders — for example, attrition is the symptom, time mismatch the cause, and mechanism design the cure. This adjacency is a finding, not a rendering bug.

4. What the network says (one line)

The highest-betweenness questions now sit on institution funding/reputation and firm value/sustainability, confirming that five-party incentive alignment and sustainability remain the research spine.
